## PLAIN-LANGUAGE SUMMARY (not a substitute for the terms below)

1. You are agreeing to let the Fab Lab make a three-dimensional digital scan of your head, face, and (optionally) neck and shoulders.
2. **You own the resulting file.** It is yours, entirely and forever. The Fab Lab does not own it, and never will.
3. Because you own it, you are *lending* the Fab Lab permission — a license — to use the file for one purpose only: printing physical replicas for teaching, learning, and research inside the School. Not for sale. Not for advertising. Not for anyone outside the Lab.
4. You get a copy of the file.
5. You may cancel the Fab Lab's permission at any time, in writing.
6. You are not being paid, and you may stop the scan at any moment for any reason.
7. Because GW's own policy would otherwise give the University a claim to files made by paid staff, a University officer must also sign §12.4 waiving that claim. **If §12.4 is blank, do not sign this form and do not sit for the scan** — your ownership in point 2 depends on it.

### 4.1 Grant

Subject, as owner, grants to the Fab Lab a **non-exclusive, royalty-free, non-transferable, non-sublicensable, revocable** license to reproduce the Scan File and to manufacture physical replicas from it, solely and exclusively for **Educational Use** as defined in §4.2, and solely for the term stated in §4.4.

### 4.2 "Educational Use" Defined

"**Educational Use**" means use for instruction, learning, demonstration, critique, technique development, equipment calibration, and non-commercial scholarly research, in every case:

(a) conducted by or under the supervision of the Fab Lab;

(d) without transfer of the Scan File itself — as distinct from a physical replica — to any person outside the Fab Lab.

### 4.3 Physical Replicas

Physical replicas produced under this license are the property of the Fab Lab (or, where a student pays material cost, of that student), **subject to** the following, which run with each replica:

(a) no replica may be sold, offered for sale, rented, auctioned, or otherwise commercially exploited;
(b) no replica may be used in advertising, promotion, fundraising, or trade without Subject's separate written consent under §6;
(c) no replica may be used in a manner that is defamatory, obscene, degrading, or that a reasonable person in Subject's position would find humiliating; and
(d) no replica may be publicly displayed under Subject's name without Subject's separate written consent under §6.

Upon revocation or expiration of the license, the Fab Lab shall, at Subject's written election, either (i) destroy all replicas in its possession or control and certify that destruction in writing, or (ii) deliver them to Subject at Subject's expense, or (iii) retain them subject to clauses (a)–(d) above, which continue indefinitely.
