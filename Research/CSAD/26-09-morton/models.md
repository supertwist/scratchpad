# Hi-Res 3D Scans of Greco-Roman Sculpture — Public Domain Sources

Institutions and initiatives that have released downloadable, freely-licensed (public domain / CC0) 3D scans of Greco-Roman antiquities. License terms are generally per-object, so confirm the individual object's license before reuse.

| Institution | Link | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Smithsonian Institution (Smithsonian 3D / Open Access) | [3d.si.edu](https://3d.si.edu/) · [si.edu/openaccess](https://www.si.edu/openaccess) | Smithsonian Open Access covers 5M+ items, including 2,000+ CC0 3D scans (OBJ/glTF) spanning natural history to classical-style sculpture (e.g., Hiram Powers' *Greek Slave*). The `3d.si.edu` collections hub lists individually downloadable models. |
| The Metropolitan Museum of Art | [metmuseum.org/hubs/open-access](https://www.metmuseum.org/hubs/open-access) | The Met's Open Access program (CC0) includes 100+ high-resolution 3D scans of works across the collection, including Greco-Roman marbles and Roman-period sculpture, downloadable as glTF/GLB. |
| The British Museum | [sketchfab.com/britishmuseum](https://sketchfab.com/britishmuseum) · [sketchfab.com/britishmuseum/models](https://sketchfab.com/britishmuseum/models) | 250+ 3D scans hosted on Sketchfab, including Greek and Roman marbles, busts, and reliefs. Many are downloadable; check each model's individual CC license (several are CC0/public domain). |
| Museum of Classical Archaeology, Cambridge / Fitzwilliam Museum | [sketchfab.com/fitzwilliammuseum/collections/classical-archaeology](https://sketchfab.com/fitzwilliammuseum/collections/classical-archaeology-1b80074ce63d43f6813fe332d0f50413) | ~48 photogrammetry scans of classical casts and originals (grave stelae, urns, sculpture) from Cambridge's collections, produced via university photogrammetry workshops and published for download. |
| Scan the World (MyMiniFactory) | [myminifactory.com/scantheworld](https://www.myminifactory.com/scantheworld/) | Community/institutional archive of 18,000+ scans of sculpture and cultural artifacts, many sourced directly from museums (including the Louvre) with a large Greco-Roman contingent; all files are free to download under Scan the World's public-domain-style terms. |

## Notes
- License scope varies by object even within a single institution's Sketchfab or Open Access hub — verify the license tag (CC0, CC-BY, etc.) on each specific model page before downloading/reuse.
- Not included: aggregators like Google Arts & Culture, which primarily offer in-browser viewers rather than direct downloadable files for most Greco-Roman objects.

## Louvre / RMN-Grand Palais

The Louvre does not host its own downloadable-3D-model site, but 3D scans of its collection are published officially through **RMN-Grand Palais** (Réunion des musées nationaux — Grand Palais), the public agency that manages reproductions and digitization for the Louvre and other French national museums.

| Source | Link | Description |
|---|---|---|
| RMN-Grand Palais official Sketchfab account | [sketchfab.com/francecollections](https://sketchfab.com/francecollections) | Official account, self-described as "Official 3D reproductions of the French national museums' collections." ~930 models, 1.1M+ views. |
| Musée du Louvre collection (on the above account) | [sketchfab.com/francecollections/collections/musee-du-louvre](https://sketchfab.com/francecollections/collections/musee-du-louvre-b3a47cd15ead4f2992ab02d6339712b6) | Curated collection of Louvre-object scans, including Greco-Roman/classical antiquities (e.g., "Cavalier Romain," "Tête de Perse") alongside Egyptian and Renaissance pieces. Download availability and license are set per model — check each model page individually; not all are free to download. |
