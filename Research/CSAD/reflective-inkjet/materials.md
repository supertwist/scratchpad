## B. Retroreflective signage vinyl (⚠ generally NOT compatible with the P9570)

These use glass-bead retroreflection (light bounces back toward its source) for vehicle graphics/safety signage. They're "inkjet printable," but that means **solvent, eco-solvent, latex, or UV-curable** wide-format inkjet — not the P9570's aqueous pigment system. Feeding self-adhesive vinyl through a P9570 is also not something Epson supports for this media class. Listed for completeness in case this is actually what you meant, at the closest width to 44":

## Notes / caveats
- Prices marked "check listing" or "~" fluctuate and weren't verifiable via a live checkout at research time — confirm at the vendor link before ordering.
- For Section A, always double-check the exact P9570 profile is in the downloaded package (Moab and Red River bundle multiple papers per printer in one zip/page) and select the matching media-type/ICC pair in the Epson driver or your RIP.
- If what you actually want is retroreflective safety sheeting that runs on the P9570 itself (aqueous pigment), that combination doesn't really exist in the market — retroreflective glass-bead sheeting is made for solvent/latex/UV printers.
