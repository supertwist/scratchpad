# Best Commentaries on Ovid's Metamorphoses

| Author & Description | Source |
|---|---|
| **Franz Bömer** — *P. Ovidius Naso: Metamorphosen, Kommentar* (Heidelberg: Winter Verlag, 1969–1986). The gold-standard scholarly commentary: seven volumes in German covering all 15 books, exhaustively annotated with ancient parallels and secondary literature. Widely cited as the indispensable reference for serious Ovidian philology, though its lack of an English translation limits accessibility. | [Bryn Mawr Classical Review on Bömer's Addenda/Corrigenda/Indices](https://bmcr.brynmawr.edu/2006/2006.10.17) |
| **Alessandro Barchiesi (general editor), with Gianpiero Rosati, E. J. Kenney, Joseph D. Reed, and Philip Hardie** — *Ovidio: Metamorfosi* (Fondazione Lorenzo Valla, "Scrittori greci e latini," 6 vols., 2005–2015); English edition *A Commentary on Ovid's Metamorphoses* (Cambridge University Press, 3 vols., 2024). The major modern multi-author commentary on the complete poem, now available in English — widely regarded as the current landmark reference. | [Cambridge UP frontmatter, Vol. 1](https://assets.cambridge.org/97805218/95798/frontmatter/9780521895798_frontmatter.pdf) |
| **William S. Anderson** — *Ovid's Metamorphoses, Books 6–10* (University of Oklahoma Press, 1972) and *Ovid's Metamorphoses, Books 1–5* (University of Oklahoma Press, 1997). The standard English-language scholarly commentary on these ten books, praised as detailed yet readable — a frequent choice for advanced undergraduate and graduate study. | [Bryn Mawr Classical Review on Books 1–5](https://bmcr.brynmawr.edu/1997/1997.09.11/) |
| **D. E. (Donald) Hill** — *Ovid: Metamorphoses*, Aris & Phillips Classical Texts, 4 vols.: Books I–IV (1985), V–VIII (1992), IX–XII (1999), XIII–XV (2000). Latin text with facing line-by-line English translation and accessible commentary notes on sources and literary/artistic influence — the most widely used English teaching edition covering the entire poem. | [Liverpool University Press / Aris & Phillips, complete set](https://www.liverpooluniversitypress.co.uk/doi/book/10.3828/9780856687372) |
| **E. J. Kenney** — introduction and notes to A. D. Melville's translation, *Ovid: Metamorphoses* (Oxford World's Classics, 1986). Not a line-by-line scholarly commentary, but Kenney's introduction and explanatory notes are a highly regarded compact entry point, and Kenney went on to write the commentary on Books 7–9 in the Fondazione Valla/Cambridge project above. | [Oxford World's Classics catalogue entry](https://global.oup.com/academic/product/metamorphoses-9780199537372) |

## Notes
- For comprehensive, all-book scholarly coverage: **Bömer** (German) remains the deepest single resource; the **Barchiesi/Cambridge** commentary is the best current option in English.
- For single-volume English study with text + translation + commentary: **Anderson** (Books 1–10) and **Hill** (all 15 books) are the standard choices.
